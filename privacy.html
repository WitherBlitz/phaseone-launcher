PhaseOne Launcher – Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · App: PhaseOne Launcher (com.wither.phaseonelauncher)

Summary

PhaseOne Launcher does not collect, sell, or share any personal data, and the developer runs no servers that receive your data. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting services. It uses the network only for features you start yourself: MultiPhase (sharing a keyboard, mouse and clipboard between your own devices on your local network) and PhaseThrough downloads of Windows runtimes from their makers' own websites. Everything else stays on your device.

What the app accesses on your device, and why

To work as a home screen and desktop, PhaseOne uses the following on your device only. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Installed apps (Query all packages): to list, search and open your apps, as any launcher does.
  • Files (All files access, only if you allow it): for the built-in file manager (PhaseFiles), photo viewer (PhotoPhase) and file search. File names are read into memory to search them; the files themselves are never uploaded.
  • Accessibility service ("PhaseOne helper", only if you turn it on): to show the desktop taskbar above apps, list open windows, respond to keyboard shortcuts (Windows key, Win+Shift+S, Win+V), resize snapped or maximized windows to leave room for the taskbar, freeze apps with Ice Box when you tap "Close all", take screenshots with PhaseSnip, and notice when you copy something for clipboard history. It does not record what you type, and nothing it observes leaves your device.
  • TV Mode KB (an on-screen keyboard, only if you turn it on in Android's keyboard settings): types the keys you pick with a remote or controller into the app you're using. It doesn't store, log, or send what you type.
  • Clipboard: if clipboard history is on, your last 50 copies (text, pictures, file references) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete them at any time with "Clear all", or turn clipboard history off in settings.
  • Screenshots: snips you take are saved to your Pictures/Screenshots folder and copied to your clipboard, on your device.
  • Audio and nearby devices (Modify audio settings, Nearby devices): to show the volume flyout, mute the microphone, and switch audio output between your phone, monitor and Bluetooth devices. No scanning for location is done.
  • Network state: to show the Wi-Fi/network icon in the taskbar.
  • Local network (Internet, Wi-Fi multicast, connected-device foreground service), only while MultiPhase is on: your devices find each other on your local network, pair with a code you confirm, and then send keyboard and mouse input, screen layout, and (if you copy something) clipboard contents directly to each other, encrypted with TLS. Nothing goes through the developer or any other server. While the "Get MultiPhase for Windows" sheet is open, the phone also serves the Windows installer to computers on your network.
  • Downloads (PhaseThrough, only when you choose to install one): Windows runtimes such as the Visual C++ and .NET redistributables are downloaded directly from Microsoft's or NVIDIA's own servers and checked against a fixed fingerprint. Those servers see an ordinary download request (your IP address), under their own privacy policies.
  • Web search: if you search the web from the search box, your search is opened in your browser (Google search), under Google's privacy policy.
  • Ice Box (if installed): to freeze and unfreeze apps you choose, through Ice Box's own interface.

Data sharing

PhaseOne does not share data with anyone, including the developer. When you choose to open or share a file with another app (for example "Open with" or "Share"), that app receives the file you picked, under its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Settings, clipboard history and desktop backgrounds you add are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling PhaseOne deletes all of it.

Children

PhaseOne is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
